About Me

Tomoaki Suzudo, Ph.D Computational Materials Scientist   [Google Scholar]  [ORCID]  [Researchgate]

I was a research scientist in computational materials at the Japan Atomic Energy Agency (JAEA) until December 2023. My specialty is computer simulation and modeling the fracture and irradiation effects of metallic materials.

I have extensive experience in the modeling and simulation of physical systems. I have authored and co-authored numerous papers in English and Japanese, and have worked on international projects with scientists in the UK and USA .

I was awarded a Ph.D. in Engineering from Tokyo Institute of Technology for a study of nuclear reactor dynamics and diagnostics. I also have a Masters degree from Tokyo Institute of Technology and a Bachelor of Science from Tokyo University of Science.
